Gatekeeper

Gatekeeper shows you where attackers can move undetected, and how to stop them. It maps every route an intruder could take from a foothold to your most critical assets, then calculates the smallest set of tripwires that puts detection on every one of those paths. Instead of scattering canary tokens and decoy credentials by guesswork, you get a precise placement plan and a clear coverage score you can track over time. Wherever an attacker moves, they trip something.
