Rubicon

Rubicon orders risky changes so you never cross a point of no return by accident. It models migrations, key rotations, and cutovers as a map of states and finds a sequence where every step can still be undone. Where a one-way step is unavoidable, it flags it in advance and shows what to prepare first. Ship big changes with an exit at every step.
