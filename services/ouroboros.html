Ouroboros

Ouroboros finds the loops that make recovery impossible. It maps what each critical service needs in order to come back, then shows where that chain circles back on itself, like a restore that depends on the identity provider that just went down. For every loop it finds, it computes the cheapest fix, such as an out-of-band path, a break-glass credential, or a second copy. You learn you can’t recover before the outage tells you.
