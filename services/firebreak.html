Firebreak

Firebreak turns breach response from guesswork into a plan. It takes your identity and network graph and computes the cheapest set of actions that cuts an attacker off from your critical assets. Each move, like disabling an account or isolating a host, is weighted by its real business cost, so responders aren’t improvising under pressure. Contain the fire without burning down the business.
