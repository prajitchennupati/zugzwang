Understudy

Understudy checks whether your failover would actually work, not whether it’s configured. It reads your cloud configuration, quotas, and dependencies to test whether the backup region could really absorb the load, down to capacity, certificates, secrets, and replicas. When the answer is no, it shows exactly which missing piece breaks the plan and what it takes to fix it. Find out your backup can’t take the stage before the night you need it.
